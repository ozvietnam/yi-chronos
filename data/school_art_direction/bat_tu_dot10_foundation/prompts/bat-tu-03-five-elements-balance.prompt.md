# bat-tu-03-five-elements-balance

Create a Five Elements balance diagram background for a Ba Zi web UI, 1:1 or 4:3 ratio.

Scene: a refined qi-balance table or material mandala made from real substances: fresh wood, cinnabar/fire, yellow earth, forged metal, black ink water. Thin engraved lines or red threads suggest generating/controlling relationships, but keep the center readable and calm for UI overlay.

Mood: premium, material, tactile, calm, not neon.

Must communicate: Five Elements in Ba Zi are qi proportions and balancing forces, not decorative colors.

Avoid: no flat game icons, no bright neon palette, no cluttered arrow chart, no readable fake text, no Tử Vi oracle border.

Output: source PNG/JPG at least 2200px long edge; web-ready WebP 1200-1400px long edge, under 300KB.
