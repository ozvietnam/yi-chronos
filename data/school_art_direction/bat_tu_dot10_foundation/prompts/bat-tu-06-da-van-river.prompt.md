# bat-tu-06-da-van-river

Create a Da Yun ten-year luck-pillar timeline illustration for a Ba Zi web UI, 21:9 or 16:9 ratio.

Scene: a river or mountain road divided into subtle ten-year segments. Each segment shifts slightly by season and Five Element atmosphere. Use stone markers or bamboo slips along the path, but no text. Flow moves left to right. Keep center or upper area clean enough for UI overlay.

Mood: long-term movement, dignified, contemplative, not fatalistic.

Must communicate: Da Yun is the long flow of ten-year qi phases; it gives context, not absolute good/bad judgment.

Avoid: no modern infographic, no health-app timeline icons, no dramatic up/down fortune graph, no readable text.

Output: source PNG/JPG at least 2200px long edge; web-ready WebP 1600px long edge, under 450KB.
