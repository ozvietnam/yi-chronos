# ky-mon-07-relative-direction-center

Create a Qimen Dunjia relative-direction illustration for a web UI, 4:3 ratio.

Scene: a bronze Qimen compass/nine-palace overlay sits on an ancient map. A bright center point represents the querent/context. Eight direction rays extend outward. A faint second overlay shows the center shifted, with rays redrawn, demonstrating that direction depends on the chosen center and real-world context.

Mood: strategic, spatial, sober, ancient map and bronze instruments, not modern GPS.

Must communicate: Qimen directions are relative to the querent/context center, not fixed absolute fortune directions.

Avoid: no Google map look, no readable direction labels, no generic feng-shui luopan overload, no modern infographic arrows.

Output: source PNG/JPG at least 2200px long edge; web-ready WebP 1200-1400px long edge, under 300KB.
