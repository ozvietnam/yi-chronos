# cdk_phi_18_khoc — Khốc 哭

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: vertical 2:3 oracle card, high-resolution raster illustration.

Primary request:
Create one oracle card for **Chiếu Đởm Kinh / 18 Phi Tinh**, card 18: **KHỐC 哭**.

Art direction:
- This is a Chiếu Đởm Kinh phi tinh, not a Tử Vi Bắc Phái character card.
- Treat Khốc as a living ritual sign: grief transformed into clarity.
- Main pháp tượng: a small ancient mourning bell suspended above a thiên bàn; silver tears fall onto the disk and turn into faint points of light.
- Yin metal energy: cold silver light moves to the right side, restrained and solemn.
- Element: kim. Use silver, cold white, blue-gray, light black, tiny muted bronze details.
- Background: dark old scripture paper, faint star tracks, thin incense smoke, quiet ritual atmosphere.
- Border: silver-gray and antique bronze, not royal gold.
- Top title: `KHỐC 哭`
- Footer verdict: `Khốc đắc sở, lệ hóa thành minh.`

Composition:
- Vertical tarot/oracle card.
- Top title clear and large, centered.
- Central symbol: bell + silver tear drops + illuminated thiên bàn.
- Footer text in a separate lower band, readable.
- Add 4 small branch glyph hints around the border: Sửu, Thân, Mão, Ngọ.

Avoid:
- No corpse, no funeral crowd, no explicit tomb, no horror mood, no gore.
- Do not make it melodramatic or frightening; it should feel sacred, cold, and transformative.
- No Hồng Loan wedding red, no Kình Dương warrior, no Bắc Phái deity.
