# cdk_phi_01_tu — Tử 紫

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: vertical 2:3 oracle card, high-resolution raster illustration.

Primary request:
Create one oracle card for **Chiếu Đởm Kinh / 18 Phi Tinh**, card 01: **TỬ 紫**.

Art direction:
- This is not Tử Vi Đế Tinh Bắc Phái. Do not draw an emperor, throne, crown, court, or royal portrait.
- Treat Tử as purple life-force beginning to rise, a seed of noble qi, not a king.
- Main pháp tượng: a violet star-seed sprouting on the axis of an ancient thiên bàn, with a thin red destiny thread circling it.
- Yang wood energy: light rises and leans leftward, fresh but solemn.
- Element: mộc. Use deep violet, dark green wood tone, jade light, antique bronze.
- Background: dark old scripture paper, compass disk, subtle star traces.
- Border: antique bronze, muted jade, gray silver; avoid heavy royal gold.
- Top title: `TỬ 紫`
- Footer verdict: `Tử khí sinh vinh, đúng cung thì hiển.`

Composition:
- Vertical tarot/oracle card.
- Top title clear and large, centered.
- Central symbol: violet star-seed + sprout + red destiny thread over thiên bàn.
- Footer text in a separate lower band, readable.
- Add 5 small branch glyph hints around the border: Thân, Tý, Tỵ, Dậu, Hợi.

Avoid:
- No emperor, no throne, no crown, no Tử Vi Bắc Phái motif.
- No person as main subject.
- No modern fantasy battle, no neon sci-fi, no watermark.
