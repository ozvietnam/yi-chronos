# cdk_phi_17_nhan — Nhận 刃

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: vertical 2:3 oracle card, high-resolution raster illustration.

Primary request:
Create one oracle card for **Chiếu Đởm Kinh / 18 Phi Tinh**, card 17: **NHẬN 刃**.

Art direction:
- This is a Chiếu Đởm Kinh phi tinh/pháp tượng, not Kình Dương Bắc Phái as a warrior.
- Treat Nhận as edge, blade, consequence, sharp turning point.
- Main pháp tượng: a thin ritual blade suspended before a star and thiên bàn, reflecting cold light; one tiny dark red bead near the edge, symbolic only.
- Yin metal energy: light is cold, narrow, moving rightward.
- Element: kim. Use polished silver, dark red, blue-black, pale steel, subdued bronze.
- Background: ancient dark scripture paper, compass lines, tiny star cuts in the air.
- Border: silver-gray and antique bronze.
- Top title: `NHẬN 刃`
- Footer verdict: `Nhận sắc nơi đúng, họa chuyển thành lành.`

Composition:
- Vertical tarot/oracle card.
- Top title clear and large, centered.
- Central symbol: blade suspended over star/thiên bàn, not held by a person.
- Footer text in a separate lower band, readable.
- Add 4 small branch glyph hints around the border: Thân, Tỵ, Ngọ, Dần.

Avoid:
- No battle scene, no armored warrior, no severed body, no blood splash, no horror.
- Do not make it look like Kình Dương Bắc Phái or a generic sword card.
- No modern weapon styling, no neon fantasy.
