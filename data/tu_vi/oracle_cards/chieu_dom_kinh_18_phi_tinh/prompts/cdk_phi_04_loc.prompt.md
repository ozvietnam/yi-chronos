# cdk_phi_04_loc — Lộc 禄

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: vertical 2:3 oracle card, high-resolution raster illustration.

Create one oracle card for Chiếu Đởm Kinh / 18 Phi Tinh, card 04: **LỘC 禄**.

Art direction: this is flowing phi tinh lộc, not Lộc Tồn as a warehouse keeper. Main pháp tượng: a green stream of tài khí flowing through sprouting grain, old coins and a small channel across an ancient thiên bàn. Yang wood energy opens leftward. Use green wood, young gold, warm brown earth, restrained bronze. Background: dark old scripture paper, faint waterline and star traces.

Top title: `LỘC 禄`
Footer verdict: `Lộc mạch khai thông, tài khí có nguồn.`
Border hints: Dần, Mão, Tỵ, Ngọ.

Avoid: no person guarding treasure, no vault keeper, no excessive gold pile, no merchant scene, no modern money.
