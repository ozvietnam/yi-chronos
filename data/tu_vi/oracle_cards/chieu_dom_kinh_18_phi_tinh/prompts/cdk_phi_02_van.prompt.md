# cdk_phi_02_van — Văn 文

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: vertical 2:3 oracle card, high-resolution raster illustration.

Primary request:
Create one oracle card for **Chiếu Đởm Kinh / 18 Phi Tinh**, card 02: **VĂN 文**.

Art direction:
- This is not Văn Xương/Văn Khúc Bắc Phái as paired scholars.
- Treat Văn as written qi: a luminous glyph, brush trace, and star-script recognition.
- Main pháp tượng: the character 文 glowing on an ancient page; brush strokes lift from the paper and become small flying stars.
- Yang wood energy: light opens leftward, elegant and clean.
- Element: mộc. Use green ink, old paper white, pale gold, antique bronze, restrained jade.
- Background: old scripture page over a faint thiên bàn, ink texture, quiet scholarly aura without human figures.
- Border: antique bronze and muted jade.
- Top title: `VĂN 文`
- Footer verdict: `Văn khí khai khoa, chữ sáng thành danh.`

Composition:
- Vertical tarot/oracle card.
- Top title clear and large, centered.
- Central symbol: glowing 文 glyph + brush + star-like ink traces.
- Footer text in a separate lower band, readable.
- Add 3 small branch glyph hints around the border: Dần, Ngọ, Tuất.

Avoid:
- No two scholars, no direct Văn Xương/Văn Khúc pair motif.
- No classroom, no modern book, no fantasy wizard, no person as main subject.
- No royal court, no emperor, no watermark.
