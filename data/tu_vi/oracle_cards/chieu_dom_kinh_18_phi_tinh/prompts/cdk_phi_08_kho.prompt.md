# cdk_phi_08_kho — Khố 库

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: vertical 2:3 oracle card, high-resolution raster illustration.

Create one oracle card for Chiếu Đởm Kinh / 18 Phi Tinh, card 08: **KHỐ 库**.

Art direction: storehouse/vault as a place, not Lộc Tồn as a person. Main pháp tượng: an ancient stone treasury door half-open, with soft tài khố light and old script tablets inside; key and lock motifs over a thiên bàn. Yang earth energy rises leftward. Use earth gold, stone gray, dark jade, muted bronze.

Top title: `KHỐ 库`
Footer verdict: `Khố giữ đúng vị, tài khí không sai.`
Border hints: Mão, Tỵ, Ngọ, Mùi, Hợi.

Avoid: no human guard, no piles of modern coins, no fantasy dragon hoard, no Lộc Tồn duplicate.
