# cdk_phi_16_hinh — Hình 刑

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: vertical 2:3 oracle card, high-resolution raster illustration.

Primary request:
Create one oracle card for **Chiếu Đởm Kinh / 18 Phi Tinh**, card 16: **HÌNH 刑**.

Art direction:
- This is a Chiếu Đởm Kinh phi tinh/pháp tượng, not a horror or execution scene.
- Treat Hình as law, consequence, red line, ritual judgment.
- Main pháp tượng: a precise red legal line cutting across an ancient thiên bàn, with a dim balance scale and ruler-shadow behind it.
- Yin fire energy: the light is red, controlled, sinking to the right.
- Element: hỏa. Use deep red, black, cold silver, muted bronze.
- Background: dark legal-scripture paper, faint court seal texture, compass lines.
- Border: antique bronze and cold silver with restrained red accents.
- Top title: `HÌNH 刑`
- Footer verdict: `Hình đúng phép, tai không thành tai.`

Composition:
- Vertical tarot/oracle card.
- Top title clear and large, centered.
- Central symbol: red line + thiên bàn + scale/ruler shadows.
- Footer text in a separate lower band, readable.
- Add 4 small branch glyph hints around the border: Dần, Dậu, Ngọ, Tuất.

Avoid:
- No torture, no execution, no blood, no corpse, no prison gore.
- No warrior, no judge-person as main subject; the law-symbol must be central.
- No modern courtroom, no fantasy battle card.
