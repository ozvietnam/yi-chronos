# cdk_phi_14_hu — Hư 虚

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: vertical 2:3 oracle card, high-resolution raster illustration.

Primary request:
Create one oracle card for **Chiếu Đởm Kinh / 18 Phi Tinh**, card 14: **HƯ 虚**.

Art direction:
- This is not Northern Tử Vi / 14 Chính Tinh. Do not draw a deity, scholar, emperor, warrior, or anime/fantasy battle character.
- Treat Hư as a living symbolic phenomenon: emptiness, hollow fame, reflection, mist, water illusion.
- Main pháp tượng: a palace reflected in a cracked bronze mirror, dissolving into blue-gray mist and faint water ripples over an ancient thiên bàn.
- Yin star energy: the light sinks and drifts to the right side of the card, quiet and cold.
- Element: thủy. Use smoky blue, pale silver, muted violet-gray, soft black.
- Background: dark old scripture paper, faint compass/thiên bàn lines, ink stains, small flying star traces.
- Border: antique bronze and gray silver, restrained, not royal gold.
- Top title: `HƯ 虚`
- Footer verdict: `Hư danh như nước, tỉnh rồi mới vinh.`

Composition:
- Vertical tarot/oracle card.
- Top title clear and large, centered.
- Central symbol occupies the middle: cracked mirror + dissolving reflected palace + mist.
- Footer text in a separate lower band, readable.
- Add 4 small branch glyph hints around the border: Ngọ, Mùi, Dậu, Hợi.

Avoid:
- No horror, ghosts, corpses, skulls, gore, or frightening haunted-house look.
- No Tử Vi emperor, no Văn Xương scholar, no Hồng Loan romance motif.
- No modern fantasy armor, no battle scene, no photorealism, no watermark.
