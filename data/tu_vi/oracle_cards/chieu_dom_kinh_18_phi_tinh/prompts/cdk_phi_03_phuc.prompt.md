# cdk_phi_03_phuc — Phúc 福

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: vertical 2:3 oracle card, high-resolution raster illustration.

Create one oracle card for Chiếu Đởm Kinh / 18 Phi Tinh, card 03: **PHÚC 福**.

Art direction: phi tinh blessing as accumulated ground, not a cheerful folk god. Main pháp tượng: the character 福 as a talisman seal hanging above a bright patch of earth; small golden seeds of phúc khí fall onto a calm doorway and field lines over a thiên bàn. Yang earth energy rises leftward and feels warm. Use earth gold, jade-brown, warm green, muted bronze.

Top title: `PHÚC 福`
Footer verdict: `Phúc đến đúng chỗ, nền đời được nâng.`
Border hints: Dần, Mão, Tỵ, Ngọ.

Avoid: no smiling folk deity, no cartoon luck god, no festive comedy, no royal court, no Bắc Phái character portrait.
