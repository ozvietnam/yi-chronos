# cdk_phi_06_tho — Thọ 寿

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: vertical 2:3 oracle card, high-resolution raster illustration.

Create one oracle card for Chiếu Đởm Kinh / 18 Phi Tinh, card 06: **THỌ 寿**.

Art direction: symbolic longevity, not Thiên Lương as a wise elder. Main pháp tượng: the character 寿 carved on an old stone beside a pine branch and small long-life lamp, all aligned over an ancient thiên bàn. Yang earth energy rises leftward, steady and durable. Use pine green, earth gold, ivory white, old stone gray, muted bronze.

Top title: `THỌ 寿`
Footer verdict: `Thọ khí bền sâu, xung hợp hanh thông.`
Border hints: Hợi, Dậu, Dần.

Avoid: no elderly sage portrait, no crane-dominated folk painting, no deity, no Bắc Phái Thiên Lương motif.
