# 82-phu-the-tu-tham-hoa-loc-trung-chau

Hero/banner ngang 16:9 cho Cung Phu The Bac Phai Trung Chau: ban co Trung Chau trong phong sach son mai toi, khe uoc hon nhan/su nghiep tren thien ban 12 cung, hai luong khi tim-vang Tu Vi va luc-do ruou Tham Lang Hoa Loc gap tai cung Mao/Phu The, an trien, chen ngoc, day do manh, hoa dao tiet che, tai nguyen kin dao; khong chu, khong romance sen, khong duc tinh.

Meaning: Tu-Tham Hoa Loc o Phu The: quan he co suc hut, chi tien thu, tai nguyen va dong hanh xay su nghiep.
