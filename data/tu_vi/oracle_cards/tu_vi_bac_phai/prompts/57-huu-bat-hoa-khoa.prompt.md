# 57 — Hữu Bật Hóa Khoa

Create one premium vertical Vietnamese Tử Vi oracle card titled "HỮU BẬT HÓA KHOA".
Single card only, tall tarot/oracle ratio 2:3, antique gold border, dark indigo lacquer texture, subtle Đông Sơn bronze motifs, refined Vietnamese-Han celestial art, painterly crisp detail.

System name: `[TU_HOA] HUU BAT HOA KHOA`
Card type: TU_HOA

Visual brief: Hữu Bật transformed by Hóa Khoa. A right-side assistant star, elegant and calm, lifts a scroll of merit and a jade brush; a ring of scholars, documents and guiding light forms behind the main figure. It should feel like support becoming recognized reputation, clear method, and cứu giải through knowledge.

Main colors: jade, ivory paper, silver, antique gold, clear blue.
Footer text: `Trợ lực thành danh, khoa minh giải nguy.`

Avoid: do not make it Văn Xương/Văn Khúc pair; Hữu Bật is the helper/support star receiving Khoa.
