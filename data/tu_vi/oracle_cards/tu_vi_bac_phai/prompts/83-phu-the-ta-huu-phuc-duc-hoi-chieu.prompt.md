# 83-phu-the-ta-huu-phuc-duc-hoi-chieu

Card ngang 4:3 cho Ta-Huu tu Phuc Duc hoi chieu Phu The: den to, gia pha, anh sang phuc duc phia sau, hai dai sang Ta Phu va Huu Bat di vao Phu The nhu hai luc nang do, an Khoa mo, khong ve to tien qua cu the, khong thanh Phuc Duc chung chung, khong chu.

Meaning: Phu The duoc Ta Phu Huu Bat va nen Phuc Duc nang do; hon nhan co hoc, co tro luc, khong dung mot minh.
